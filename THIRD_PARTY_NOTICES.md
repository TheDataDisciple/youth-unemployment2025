## International Labour Organization

**Source:** International Labour Organization, *Global Employment Trends for Youth 2026: Back to the future*. The analysis uses the corrected accessible PDF dated 31 August 2026.

The Excel workbook, Power BI report, chart PNG, README and LinkedIn post reproduce selected ILO modelled estimates for 2016–2025 and the percentage-point changes printed in Figure 1.3. The public narrative notes a global youth unemployment rate of 12.4% in 2025, approximately 67 million unemployed young people, and six increases, two reported rounded zeros and three decreases among eleven subregions between 2023 and 2025. A rounded zero does not establish exact stability or statistical significance. World is a benchmark excluded from that subregion count.

The values were manually transcribed from Table A.1 and Figure 1.3. Rates are stored as decimal fractions in Excel and displayed as percentages; reported changes are stored as percentage points. The workbook and report add the author's formatting and presentation; they are not official ILO outputs. Missing regional counts were left blank.
