**Source:** International Labour Organization, *Global Employment Trends for Youth 2026: Back to the future* and the accompanying news release, published 11 August 2026.

- [Direct source for the values used here](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/youth-unemployment-rises-young-people-face-harder-road-decent-work)

The Excel workbook, Power BI report, README and LinkedIn post reproduce selected 2025 estimates: world youth unemployment of 12.4%, Northern America 9.8%, Northern, Southern and Western Europe 15.0%, and approximately 67 million unemployed young people worldwide. The post also cites increases in eight of eleven subregions between 2023 and 2025.

The values were manually selected and transcribed from the news release. Rates are stored as decimal fractions in Excel and displayed as percentages. The workbook and report add the author's formatting and presentation; they are not official ILO outputs. Missing regional counts were left blank.
