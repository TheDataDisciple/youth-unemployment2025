## International Labour Organization

**Source:** International Labour Organization, *Global Employment Trends for Youth 2026: Back to the future*. The analysis uses the corrected accessible PDF dated 31 August 2026.

- [Report publication page](https://www.ilo.org/publications/major-publications/global-employment-trends-youth-2026-back-future)
- [Corrected accessible report PDF](https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2026-09/9789220437612_PDFA_Web_ENG.pdf)
- [ILO rights and permissions](https://www.ilo.org/rights-and-permissions)
- [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The Excel workbook, Power BI report, README and LinkedIn post reproduce selected ILO modelled estimates for 2016–2025 and the percentage-point changes printed in Figure 1.3. The public narrative notes a global youth unemployment rate of 12.4% in 2025, approximately 67 million unemployed young people, and that six of eleven subregions increased while two were unchanged between 2023 and 2025.

The values were manually transcribed from Table A.1 and Figure 1.3. Rates are stored as decimal fractions in Excel and displayed as percentages; reported changes are stored as percentage points. The workbook and report add the author's formatting and presentation; they are not official ILO outputs. Missing regional counts were left blank.
